The MAC Prompt Framework

This is your personal AI communication framework. Use this every time you write prompts.

M — MINDSET

Define the emotional energy.

Example: bold, cinematic, founder-energy, boss-lady, reflective, intelligent, internet-native, emotionally raw.

A — AUDIENCE

Tell AI who the content is for.

Example: Gen Z students, RBI aspirants, ambitious women, creators, startup founders, finance audience.

C — CONTEXT

Explain the backstory and objective.

AI gets dramatically better when context is detailed. Mention your journey, your struggles, goals and why the output matters.
